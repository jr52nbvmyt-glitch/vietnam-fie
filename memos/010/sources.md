[S1] "STEP provides noncommissioned officers roadmap to promotion" — U.S. Army (army.mil), 19 Feb 2016, https://www.army.mil/article/162606/step_provides_noncommissioned_officers_roadmap_to_promotion — Select, Train, Educate, Promote in force from 1 Jan 2016: Basic Leader Course for sergeant, Advanced Leader Course for staff sergeant, Senior Leader Course for sergeant first class, Master Leader Course for master sergeant and first sergeant, Sergeants Major Course for sergeant major; the Executive Leader Course for general-officer senior enlisted advisers; about 14,000 NCOs promoted without their required education in the "black log"; the Academic Evaluation Report revised to grade writing.
[S2] "Shaping the Army's extended Basic Leader Course" — U.S. Army (army.mil), 21 May 2026, https://www.army.mil/article/292757/shaping_the_armys_extended_basic_leader_course — the new 29-day Basic Leader Course replacing the 23-day course; six or seven added days of field training, eight days in the field: leader stakes lanes on skill level 10 tasks (medical, weapons, patrolling, vehicle recovery), land navigation and a culminating situational training exercise with fragmentary orders, planning and briefing; validated 28 Apr–26 May 2026 by the 166th Regiment; to be implemented across the force later in 2026; "nothing from the 23-day course is being lost".
[S3] "Building Leaders From Doctrine: The NCO Journey From BLC to SGM-A" — U.S. Army (army.mil), 31 Mar 2025, https://www.army.mil/article/284281/building_leaders_from_doctrine_the_nco_journey_from_blc_to_sgm_a — the 2025 sequence BLC, ALC, SLC, MLC and the Sergeants Major Academy under the NCO Leadership Center of Excellence at Fort Bliss; doctrine taught through real case studies; MLC aimed at operational-level leadership, policy and strategy; the Academy at strategic leadership.
[S4] "Army Set to Overhaul Length of Noncommissioned Officer Academies to Cut Costs" — Military.com, 15 Jul 2025, https://www.military.com/daily-news/2025/07/15/army-set-overhaul-length-of-noncommissioned-officer-academies-cut-costs.html — ALC and SLC, previously 8–16 weeks by specialty, to run three weeks regardless of specialty; BLC from three weeks to six with land navigation; MLC from two weeks to three; the Sergeants Major Academy unchanged at 10 months; the purpose to cut travel costs; prior training prerequisites for sergeant removed and BLC made a prerequisite for staff sergeant by the Sergeant Major of the Army; to be finalised in weeks.
[S5] Noncommissioned officer candidate course — Wikipedia, https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Noncommissioned_officer_candidate_course — Skill Development Base programme from August 1967; the NCO Candidate Course September 1967–1972, 12 weeks at Fort Benning plus 9 weeks on the job; about 33,000 NCOs trained; the critical shortage of junior NCOs after the 1965 build-up without reserve mobilisation, the 12-month tour, separations of senior NCOs and the 25-month stateside stabilisation; Basic NCO Course piloted July 1970 at Fort Sill, first Army-wide basic courses May 1971, first advanced courses January 1972, NCO Education System from late 1971.
[S6] "Army NCO History (Part 7): Korean and Vietnam Wars" — U.S. Army (army.mil), https://www.army.mil/article/18049/army_nco_history_part_7_korean_and_vietnam_wars — over 180,000 soldiers through an NCO academy by 1959; Army-wide academy standards from 1958 under AR 350-90; the NCO Candidate Course at Benning, Knox and Sill, graduates promoted E-5 and the top 5% E-6, received with mixed feelings; the first Sergeant Major of the Army, William O. Wooldridge, 1966; command sergeant major position 1967; squad- and platoon-sized operations led by NCOs under decentralised command; NCOES first courses Basic, Advanced and the Sergeants Major Academy.
[S7] MARADMIN 630/24, "Updated Enlisted Professional Military Education (EPME) Requirements for Active Duty and Active Reserve Marines" — U.S. Marine Corps, 23 Dec 2024, https://www.marines.mil/News/Messages/Messages-Display/Article/4015948/updated-enlisted-professional-military-education-epme-requirements-for-active-d/ — required EPME by grade: corporal, the resident Corporals Course and its distance programme; sergeant, Sergeants School resident or seminar and its distance programme; staff sergeant, the Career School distance programme; gunnery sergeant, the SNCO Leadership School resident or seminar and the Advanced School distance programme; first sergeant, the First Sergeants School; master gunnery sergeant, enlisted joint PME; sergeants major, the Cornerstone and slated-leader courses.

[S9] Chapter II, "The Crucial Years, 1960–1964", in Collins (as S8), https://webdoc.sub.gwdg.de/ebook/p/2005/CMH_2/www.army.mil/cmh-pg/books/vietnam/devtrainrvn/ch2.htm — a shortage of good junior leaders among the main causes of marginal ARVN effectiveness in 1964; a revised leader programme under consideration in 1964 of seven weeks for squad leaders and ten for platoon leaders, with no final action recorded; Popular Forces leadership training up to 60% behind programme and quotas never filled in 1964; poor training or none a continual handicap.
