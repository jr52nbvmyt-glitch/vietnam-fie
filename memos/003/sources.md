# Sources — Memorandum 003

One line per source, numbered in order of first use. Cite as [S1], [S2] … in the text.

[S1] "U-2S Dragon Lady" — Air & Space Forces Magazine weapons file, https://www.airandspaceforces.com/weapons/u-2 — U-2S as the Air Force's only manned high-altitude long-endurance ISR platform with SIGINT, IMINT and MASINT; ASARS-2A radar (Raytheon), SYERS-2C multispectral EO/IR (UTC Aerospace), ASIP SIGINT (Northrop Grumman); optical bar camera operations from Beale ended 2022; Block 20 glass cockpit and updated datalinks, 2023 avionics refresh; F118-GE-101 turbofan of 17,000 lb thrust; wingspan 105 ft, length 63 ft, height 16 ft, maximum take-off weight 40,000 lb; speed 410 mph; range 7,000+ miles; ceiling above 70,000 ft; 14-hour record endurance sortie of 31 July 2025; one pilot (TU-2S two); production 35, inventory 27 U-2S and 4 TU-2S; Beale AFB with forward locations at Osan, Akrotiri and Fairford; U-2R first flight 1967, TR-1 1981, U-2S re-engining from 1994; retirement proposals of 2024–26
[S2] "RQ-4 Global Hawk" — Air & Space Forces Magazine weapons file, https://www.airandspaceforces.com/weapons/rq-4 — system of aircraft, Launch and Recovery Element and Mission Control Element; Block 40 (RQ-4B) as sole remaining USAF variant with MP-RTIP AESA radar for SAR and GMTI; IOC Block 40 August 2016; production 45, inventory 9 Block 40; Grand Forks AFB with forward locations at Andersen, Sigonella, Fairford and Yokota; wingspan 130.9 ft, length 47.6 ft, height 15.3 ft; maximum take-off weight 32,250 lb; payload 3,000 lb; F137-RR-100 of 7,600 lb thrust; maximum speed 356.5 mph; range 14,150 miles; endurance 32+ hours, 24 hours on station at 1,200 miles; ceiling 60,000 ft; LRE pilot, MCE pilot and MCE sensor operator
[S3] "RQ-4 Global Hawk" fact sheet — Air Combat Command, current as of 24 January 2023, https://www.acc.af.mil/About-Us/Fact-Sheets/Display/Article/3283322/rq-4-global-hawk — all-weather day-and-night persistent near-real-time IMINT and MTI collection; fleet fielded as Block 40; Block 10 retired 2011; Block 20 converted to EQ-4 BACN relays
[S4] "4080th Strategic Wing" — Wikipedia, https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/4080th_Strategic_Wing — President Johnson's order of 31 December 1963 for U-2 reconnaissance support in South Vietnam; three 4028th SRS U-2Es as Operating Location 20 at Bien Hoa from 5 March 1964 after the Philippine government refused them; SIGINT along the North Vietnamese and Chinese borders; monitoring of roads and trails into South Vietnam, Laos and Cambodia; MiG threat forcing higher altitudes and SA-2 sites forcing route planning; Blue Springs drone detachment becoming the 4025th Reconnaissance Squadron in July 1965; wing elements at Bien Hoa to 25 June 1966
[S5] "The U-2 in Desert Storm, Chapter 3: The U-2 and its Sensors" — Federation of American Scientists, https://irp.fas.org/program/collect/u2ds/part04.htm — U-2R above 65,000 ft; can stay airborne more than 12 hours but missions normally no longer than 9; not air-refuellable; SYERS dual-band EO and IR; SYERS and ASARS-II line-of-sight ("on-tether") range to the ground station about 220 miles, stored and relayed beyond it; ASARS-II sees through cloud, smoke and darkness; H-camera 66-inch focal length; IRIS-III optical bar camera 24-inch, 140° scan, 32 nm each side; Senior Blade ground van; mobile processing element of 15 vans needing at least 14 days' contract maintenance before deployment
[S6] "RQ-4B Global Hawk High-Altitude Long-Endurance Unmanned Aerial System" — Director, Operational Test and Evaluation, FY2014 Annual Report, https://www.dote.osd.mil/Portals/97/pub/reports/FY2014/af/2014globalhawk.pdf — Block 40 MP-RTIP designed to collect imagery of stationary targets and track moving targets simultaneously; SAR image-quality problems found in the FY13 evaluation and sensor-stability problems persisting; Global Hawk uses line-of-sight and beyond-line-of-sight satellite datalinks for control and data, with direct line-of-sight downlink to forward users; ground segment of Mission Control Elements, Launch and Recovery Elements and distributed processing; Block 30 ground station, air vehicle and communications deficiencies
[S7] "Northrop Grumman RQ-4 Global Hawk" — Wikipedia, https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northrop_Grumman_RQ-4_Global_Hawk — inertial navigation aided by GPS; differential GPS corrections from the Launch and Recovery Element for take-off and landing; military X-band satellite link to the MCE; common data link for direct line-of-sight downlink; ground segment in military shelters; flyaway cost US$60.9 million (2001) and US$131.4 million (2013); cost per flight hour US$40,600 (2010) and US$18,900 (mid-2013); empty weight 14,950 lb; cruise 357 mph; endurance 34+ hours; record of crashes from 1999 including the loss of four of the early prototypes
[S8] "Ryan Model 147" — Wikipedia, https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ryan_Model_147 — first Lightning Bug overflight of China 20 August 1964; first mission from Bien Hoa 11 October 1964; 3,435 sorties against China, North Vietnam and North Korea, from 20 in 1964 to 570 in 1972; 578 drones lost; DC-130 launch and control; mid-air parachute recovery by CH-3E, 2,655 of 2,745 attempts (97%); 147B to about 62,500 ft; 147S image of a 60-mile strip at about 1 ft resolution; United Effort 147Es to South Vietnam October 1965, SA-2 fuze data relayed 13 February 1966
[S9] "Air Force asks Raytheon to build long-range ASARS-2 imaging radar for U-2 Dragon Lady reconnaissance jet" — Military & Aerospace Electronics, 13 September 2022, https://militaryaerospace.com/sensors/article/14282650/imaging-radar-longrange — ASARS-2 multimode real-time all-weather day-night long-range mapping; detects and locates stationary and moving ground targets in search and spotlight modes; wideband datalink to a ground display; very high resolution from long stand-off; ASARS-2B AESA front end with nearly double the range of ASARS-2A; contract to August 2027
[S10] "ASARS-2" — Wikipedia, https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ASARS-2 — Hughes development in the early 1980s; ASARS Improvement Program and first ASARS-2A delivered 2001; use for target location and damage assessment in Desert Storm
[S11] "SENIOR YEAR Electro-Optical Reconnaissance System [SYERS]" — Federation of American Scientists, https://irp.fas.org/program/collect/syers.htm — SYERS as the U-2's daylight, fair-weather electro-optical imaging sensor providing near-real-time imagery; dual-band, with a multispectral upgrade to improve performance at night and in fog or haze; long focal length, "deep look"
[S12] Gatesman, Beaudoin, Giles, Waldman and Nixon, "VHF/UHF Imagery and RCS Measurements of Ground Targets in Forested Terrain" — DTIC ADA461584, August 2002, https://apps.dtic.mil/sti/pdfs/ADA461584.pdf — foliage-penetration systems rely on frequencies below 1 GHz to penetrate forest canopies; tree trunks remain large scatterers even at low frequency
