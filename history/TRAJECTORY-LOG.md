# Trajectory Log

One entry per memorandum: how its provision shifted the course of the war, and where the end state moved. Entries are appended by `scripts/fie.py` from the memorandum's log block and never edited. Read top to bottom, this is the arc of the war across all provisions.

## Memo 001 — Accelerated land-to-the-tiller reform and an enforced GVN anti-corruption programme

Memorandum 001 gave the Republic land reform and an anti-corruption commission on the war's first day. The war's interior moved; its outline did not. About 1,150,000 hectares were titled by 1971, six years ahead of history, and where the government held the night its arrival became a guarantee rather than the landlord's return: the Delta's Front recruited less, filled its battalions with northerners sooner and in 1969 conceded the land issue. The Commission counted the army honestly, convicted a general and audited Phoenix, then lost its American lever with the drawdown and was curbed in 1971. Every main-force battle, Tet's political verdict, the Easter Offensive and the fall of Saigon on 30 April 1975 stand. The end state stays at rung (a). The provisions first touched G-1, the political deficit, and showed that moving it is necessary but not sufficient: a South with more to hold still needs the means to hold it against a conventional invasion after the Americans leave.
