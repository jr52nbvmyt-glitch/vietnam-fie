# Recurring persons

People who recur in the chronicle, historical or invented. Appended by `scripts/fie.py` from persons blocks. A later entry for the same person updates the earlier one.

Format: `- **<Name>** · <role> · <origin: HIST or invented in <work>> · <one line>`
- **Truong Vinh Khoa** · Commissioner Against Corruption from 1 January 1964, formerly a judge of the Saigon Court of Appeal · invented in 001 · Refused to charge the Dalat generals without evidence, convicted a general in 1967, audited Phoenix from 1968, kept in office but placed under the presidential Inspectorate in 1971
- **Phan Duy Khiem** · Brigadier General, IV Corps deputy commander for logistics 1965–67 · invented in 001 · Convicted by the Special Court on 18 April 1967 of ration and fuel diversion and sentenced to eight years
- **Vo Dinh Cuong** · Director General of National Police 1 January 1964 – 12 March 1966, formerly an inspector of the Sûreté · invented in 002 · Detained in Khanh's coup and restored in three days, kept the police out of the September 1964 coup, removed by Ky's decree in March 1966 and replaced by Loan
- **Dinh Quang Tuyen** · Judge of the Court of Cassation, chairman of the Provincial Administration Selection Board from 1 January 1964 · invented in 002 · Published the first list on 27 April 1964; the Board he chaired outlived four governments and was packed by Thieu in 1970
- **Ha Thuc Lan** · Lieutenant Colonel, engineer officer, province chief of Long An from June 1964 · invented in 002 · Placed third in the first examination; planned Operation NIGHT LANTERN with the rule that no post was settled without its platoon and a reaction within the hour
- **Le Cong Phuong** · Major, district chief of Can Giuoc from 1964, a native of Can Duoc · invented in 002 · Co-planned NIGHT LANTERN and returned the 1964 titles to their families in April 1966
- **Tran Van Sang** · Major, district chief of Mo Cay, Kien Hoa, June 1964 – November 1965 · invented in 002 · Killed by a command-detonated mine on 14 November 1965, the Front's "most harmful" district chief in the province
- **Harold Kessling** · Major, later Lieutenant Colonel, USAF; chief of the radar and imagery exploitation cell at Tan Son Nhut from 1964 · invented in 003 · Had the watch on the night of 4 August 1964 and reported no boats near the destroyers; built the Sihanoukville count night by night from 1965 to the joint estimate of March 1967
- **Arthur L. Venn** · Lieutenant Colonel, US Army; chief of the MACV small-arms new-equipment training team from January 1964 · invented in 004 · Ran Operation IRON GATE under the rule that no new weapon left a depot until the old one came in and no carbine went to a post unzeroed; refused to shorten the course for the corps commanders
- **Lam Van Thien** · Lieutenant Colonel, province chief of Chuong Thien in 1966 · invented in 004 · Protested in writing the corps' borrowing of his Regional Force companies in April 1966; his returns of the posts lost became the case for the 1967 rule that RF companies belong to their province
- **Truong Vinh Khoa** · Commissioner Against Corruption from 1 January 1964, formerly a judge of the Saigon Court of Appeal · invented in 001 · Refused to charge the Dalat generals without evidence, convicted the Sa Dec ammunition ring in 1967 on cartridge lot numbers and a general the same year, audited Phoenix from 1968, kept in office but placed under the presidential Inspectorate in 1971
- **Edwin T. Sorrell** · Major, later Lieutenant Colonel, USAF; chief of the coordinate cell at Tan Son Nhut from March 1964 to its disbandment in September 1973, formerly a geodesist of the Aeronautical Chart and Information Center · invented in 005 · Found the map datum error and ruled that no map coordinate would be given to a guided bomb; built the folders of 1964–65 and ran the siege box at Con Thien, Khe Sanh, Lam Son 719, An Loc and Kontum
- **Tran Dinh Hieu** · Lieutenant Colonel, later Colonel, Military Security; director of the national Phung Hoang centre 1 January 1964 – February 1970 · invented in 006 · Kept the centres behind the posts and refused them to provinces without night security; withdrew the raid files after Duc Thanh; resigned when the palace moved the file in 1970
- **Martin J. Ashby** · Lieutenant Colonel, US Army; chief of the American Phung Hoang cell and exploitation detachment from 1964 · invented in 006 · Built the charts of the district committees from every arrest and rallier; supported Hieu's refusal of centres to the contested provinces
- **Samuel J. Whitcomb** · Captain, US Army, Selected Advisory Corps; subsector adviser of Can Giuoc October 1964 – April 1966 · invented in 006 · Served through the aftermath of the killings and NIGHT LANTERN; his report of April 1966 said the committee's arrest, not the sweep, broke the tax
- **Ngo Van Tam** · Captain, Special Branch; chief of the Hoc Mon DIOCC from July 1964 · invented in 006 · Planned Operation STILL WATER, which broke the Hoc Mon district committee in 1965
- **Carl E. Haney** · Major, US Army, Selected Advisory Corps; subsector adviser of Dien Ban, Quang Nam, 1965–67 · invented in 006 · Walked the relieving Marine companies over their ground in August 1966 and gave them the PF leaders who reported the R-20's withdrawal
- **Walter K. Dorsey** · Lieutenant Colonel, USMC; commanding officer, 1st Battalion, 26th Marines, 1965–67 · invented in 006 · Brought the battalion to Dien Ban as a unit trained together for nine months; held the breach at La Huan on 18–19 August 1966
- **Phan Khac Minh** · Brigadier General; chairman of the Officer Selection and Promotion Board from 1 January 1964, formerly head of the General Staff's personnel directorate 1955–57 · invented in 007 · Sent to a training camp by Diem for refusing to promote the Can Lao's men; struck the bargain with Khanh in February 1964 that confirmed eleven of twenty-three coup promotions and refused twelve
- **Robert E. Lindqvist** · Colonel, US Army; chief of the American advisory cell to the Officer Selection Board from 1964 · invented in 007 · Built the evaluation system's trainers' course at Fort Benning; estimated in 1966 that half the sale of commands had ended and half had changed its name
- **Bui Quang Trieu** · ARVN officer commissioned from Thu Duc in 1957; major commanding 2nd Battalion, 9th Regiment, 5th Division from 1965; colonel commanding a 5th Division regiment from 1968 and the 3rd Division's 57th Regiment from 1971; Dalat faculty from 1972 · invented in 007 · Walked his flanks on Route 13 on 4 May 1966 and the 272nd declined its ambush; refused to sign the colonels' petition; held the Thach Han bridges at Quang Tri on 1 May 1972; led the Dalat seminar that wrote the reduced-resources study of November 1974
- **Dang Huu Thoi** · Lieutenant Colonel, commander of the VC 272nd Regiment in 1966 · invented in 007 · Withdrew the regiment from its ambush in the Xa Cam rubber on 4 May 1966 without firing, and ambushed an RF convoy at Bau Bang on 21 May
- **Vu Ngoc Hoan** · Colonel, commander of the ARVN 25th Division from March 1967 · invented in 007 · The list's second name, chosen by Thieu under the aid condition against Ky's candidate; put the division's battalions into the Hau Nghia districts at night and cut its desertion by about a quarter
- **Truong Cong Hy** · Lieutenant, clerk of the Officer Selection Board's secretariat 1965–67 · invented in 007 · Copied every command list for the B2 Front's military intelligence; arrested on 11 October 1967
- **Nguyen Dac Khiet** · Colonel, commanding a regiment of the 9th Division in 1970 · invented in 007 · Drafted the colonels' petition of September 1970 for the restoration of the general-officer list; moved to the Dalat faculty in the purge of December 1970
