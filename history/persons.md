# Recurring persons

People who recur in the chronicle, historical or invented. Appended by `scripts/fie.py` from persons blocks. A later entry for the same person updates the earlier one.

Format: `- **<Name>** · <role> · <origin: HIST or invented in <work>> · <one line>`

- **Phan Duy Khiem** · Brigadier General, IV Corps deputy commander for logistics 1965–67 · invented in 001 · Convicted by the Special Court on 18 April 1967 of ration and fuel diversion and sentenced to eight years
- **Vo Dinh Cuong** · Director General of National Police 1 January 1964 – 12 March 1966, formerly an inspector of the Sûreté · invented in 002 · Detained in Khanh's coup and restored in three days, kept the police out of the September 1964 coup, removed by Ky's decree in March 1966 and replaced by Loan
- **Dinh Quang Tuyen** · Judge of the Court of Cassation, chairman of the Provincial Administration Selection Board from 1 January 1964 · invented in 002 · Published the first list on 27 April 1964; the Board he chaired outlived four governments and was packed by Thieu in 1970
- **Ha Thuc Lan** · Lieutenant Colonel, engineer officer, province chief of Long An from June 1964 · invented in 002 · Placed third in the first examination; planned Operation NIGHT LANTERN with the rule that no post was settled without its platoon and a reaction within the hour
- **Le Cong Phuong** · Major, district chief of Can Giuoc from 1964, a native of Can Duoc · invented in 002 · Co-planned NIGHT LANTERN and returned the 1964 titles to their families in April 1966
- **Tran Van Sang** · Major, district chief of Mo Cay, Kien Hoa, June 1964 – November 1965 · invented in 002 · Killed by a command-detonated mine on 14 November 1965, the Front's "most harmful" district chief in the province
- **Harold Kessling** · Major, later Lieutenant Colonel, USAF; chief of the radar and imagery exploitation cell at Tan Son Nhut from 1964 · invented in 003 · Had the watch on the night of 4 August 1964 and reported no boats near the destroyers; built the Sihanoukville count night by night from 1965 to the joint estimate of March 1967
- **Arthur L. Venn** · Lieutenant Colonel, US Army; chief of the MACV small-arms new-equipment training team from January 1964 · invented in 004 · Ran Operation IRON GATE under the rule that no new weapon left a depot until the old one came in and no carbine went to a post unzeroed; refused to shorten the course for the corps commanders
- **Lam Van Thien** · Lieutenant Colonel, province chief of Chuong Thien in 1966 · invented in 004 · Protested in writing the corps' borrowing of his Regional Force companies in April 1966; his returns of the posts lost became the case for the 1967 rule that RF companies belong to their province
- **Truong Vinh Khoa** · Commissioner Against Corruption from 1 January 1964, formerly a judge of the Saigon Court of Appeal · invented in 001 · Refused to charge the Dalat generals without evidence, convicted the Sa Dec ammunition ring in 1967 on cartridge lot numbers and a general the same year, audited Phoenix from 1968, kept in office but placed under the presidential Inspectorate in 1971
- **Edwin T. Sorrell** · Major, later Lieutenant Colonel, USAF; chief of the coordinate cell at Tan Son Nhut from March 1964 to its disbandment in September 1973, formerly a geodesist of the Aeronautical Chart and Information Center · invented in 005 · Found the map datum error and ruled that no map coordinate would be given to a guided bomb; built the folders of 1964–65 and ran the siege box at Con Thien, Khe Sanh, Lam Son 719, An Loc and Kontum
- **Tran Dinh Hieu** · Lieutenant Colonel, later Colonel, Military Security; director of the national Phung Hoang centre 1 January 1964 – February 1970 · invented in 006 · Kept the centres behind the posts and refused them to provinces without night security; withdrew the raid files after Duc Thanh; resigned when the palace moved the file in 1970
- **Martin J. Ashby** · Lieutenant Colonel, US Army; chief of the American Phung Hoang cell and exploitation detachment from 1964 · invented in 006 · Built the charts of the district committees from every arrest and rallier; supported Hieu's refusal of centres to the contested provinces
- **Samuel J. Whitcomb** · Captain, US Army, Selected Advisory Corps; subsector adviser of Can Giuoc October 1964 – April 1966 · invented in 006 · Served through the aftermath of the killings and NIGHT LANTERN; his report of April 1966 said the committee's arrest, not the sweep, broke the tax
- **Ngo Van Tam** · Captain, Special Branch; chief of the Hoc Mon DIOCC from July 1964 · invented in 006 · Planned Operation STILL WATER, which broke the Hoc Mon district committee in 1965
- **Carl E. Haney** · Major, US Army, Selected Advisory Corps; subsector adviser of Dien Ban, Quang Nam, 1965–67 · invented in 006 · Walked the relieving Marine companies over their ground in August 1966 and gave them the PF leaders who reported the R-20's withdrawal
- **Walter K. Dorsey** · Lieutenant Colonel, USMC; commanding officer, 1st Battalion, 26th Marines, 1965–67 · invented in 006 · Brought the battalion to Dien Ban as a unit trained together for nine months; held the breach at La Huan on 18–19 August 1966
- **Phan Khac Minh** · Brigadier General; chairman of the Officer Selection and Promotion Board from 1 January 1964, formerly head of the General Staff's personnel directorate 1955–57 · invented in 007 · Sent to a training camp by Diem for refusing to promote the Can Lao's men; struck the bargain with Khanh in February 1964 that confirmed eleven of twenty-three coup promotions and refused twelve
- **Robert E. Lindqvist** · Colonel, US Army; chief of the American advisory cell to the Officer Selection Board from 1964 · invented in 007 · Built the evaluation system's trainers' course at Fort Benning; estimated in 1966 that half the sale of commands had ended and half had changed its name
- **Bui Quang Trieu** · ARVN officer commissioned from Thu Duc in 1957; major commanding 2nd Battalion, 9th Regiment, 5th Division from 1965; colonel commanding a 5th Division regiment from 1968 and the 3rd Division's 57th Regiment from 1971; Dalat faculty from 1972 · invented in 007 · Walked his flanks on Route 13 on 4 May 1966 and the 272nd declined its ambush; refused to sign the colonels' petition; held the Thach Han bridges at Quang Tri on 1 May 1972; led the Dalat seminar that wrote the reduced-resources study of November 1974
- **Dang Huu Thoi** · Lieutenant Colonel, commander of the VC 272nd Regiment in 1966 · invented in 007 · Withdrew the regiment from its ambush in the Xa Cam rubber on 4 May 1966 without firing, and ambushed an RF convoy at Bau Bang on 21 May
- **Vu Ngoc Hoan** · Colonel, commander of the ARVN 25th Division from March 1967 · invented in 007 · The list's second name, chosen by Thieu under the aid condition against Ky's candidate; put the division's battalions into the Hau Nghia districts at night and cut its desertion by about a quarter
- **Truong Cong Hy** · Lieutenant, clerk of the Officer Selection Board's secretariat 1965–67 · invented in 007 · Copied every command list for the B2 Front's military intelligence; arrested on 11 October 1967
- **Nguyen Dac Khiet** · Colonel, commanding a regiment of the 9th Division in 1970 · invented in 007 · Drafted the colonels' petition of September 1970 for the restoration of the general-officer list; moved to the Dalat faculty in the purge of December 1970
- **Van Tien Dung** · General, PAVN Chief of the General Staff, commander of the Ho Chi Minh Campaign · HIST · Set the assault on Saigon for the last days of April 1975, when the 1st Corps would be in place and the rains still a fortnight off; in this war told Hoang Cam to go round Xuan Loc on 11 April, three days earlier than historically
- **Hoang Cam** · Major General, commander of the PAVN 4th Corps 1974–75 · HIST · Took Phuoc Long in December 1974; at Xuan Loc stopped assaulting the 18th Division after three days and cut the town off, so that its twelve days were his schedule, not the defence's
- **Tran Van Tra** · Lieutenant General, commander of the B-2 Front · HIST · Argued in late 1974 for the attack on Phuoc Long as a test of the American response, against caution in Hanoi, and won
- **Nguyen Khoa Nam** · Major General, commander of IV Corps 1974–75 · HIST · Held IV Corps whole to 30 April, obeyed Minh's order to lay down arms, and took his own life on 1 May 1975
- **Ly Tong Ba** · Brigadier General, commander of the 23rd Division at Kontum in 1972 and the 25th Division in 1975 · HIST · Held Kontum in 1972; held Cu Chi and Route 1 west of Saigon in April 1975 until the 3rd Corps came on him, and was captured
- **Pham Van Phu** · Major General, commander of II Corps 1974–75 · HIST · Discounted the warnings of the blow at Ban Me Thuot and kept his forces at Pleiku; executed the retreat down Route 7
- **Huynh Cong Tri** · Major, district chief of Long My, Chuong Thien, from 1966, a list chief · invented in MT01 · Kept all forty-one of his posts through the offensive of 1972 and lost the road to Vi Thanh for seven weeks; his reports became CORDS's model of a district under siege
- **Dao Van Luong** · Major, district chief of Hoai Nhon, Binh Dinh, from 1966 · invented in MT01 · Burned the Bong Son Special Branch files on 22 April 1972, three hours before the town fell to the PAVN 3rd Division
- **Vuong Dinh Toan** · Director of Defence Industry 1 January 1964 – February 1970, a metallurgist trained at the École Centrale who built the Bien Hoa melt shop · invented in 008 · Ran the Directorate through the domestic share, the Da Nhim towers and the Phu Cat lot; replaced by a general of the President's staff in February 1970
- **Howard B. Teague** · Colonel, US Army Ordnance Corps; chief of the advisory element to the Directorate of Defence Industry from 1964 · invented in 008 · Proposed in November 1964 the domestic share that the Aid Compliance Board adopted on 1 March 1965
- **Le Van Muoi** · Sergeant, commander of the PF platoon at the Ap My Loi post, My Phuoc Tay, Cai Lay, Dinh Tuong · invented in 008 · Called the district on the night of 9 March 1967; his post held every attack for the rest of the war
- **Nguyen Thanh Liem** · Major, district chief of Cai Lay, Dinh Tuong, from 1965, a list chief · invented in 008 · Sent the 247th RF Company up the canal road on 9 March 1967 and lost 31 men at the culvert; wrote the rule that a post's first call is for fire and reliefs move by water or at dawn
- **Vu Xuan Dac** · Brigadier General, of the President's military staff; Director of Defence Industry from February 1970 · invented in 008 · Took the Directorate's contracts, licences and deferment list into the palace's gift; left Saigon on 28 April 1975
- **Daniel R. Kearsley** · Major, US Army, Selected Advisory Corps; subsector adviser of Long My, Chuong Thien, 1969–71 · invented in 008 · Handed the call for fire to the district's territorial gun platoon in 1970–71 and listened without speaking
- **Huynh Cong Tri** · Major, district chief of Long My, Chuong Thien, from 1966, a list chief · invented in MT01 · Took the call for fire into Vietnamese hands with the district's gun platoon in 1970–71; kept all forty-one of his posts through the offensive of 1972 and lost the road to Vi Thanh for seven weeks
- **Le Duan** · First Secretary of the Workers' Party of Vietnam · HIST · Presided over the Politburo conference of 30 September – 8 October 1974 that resolved on the two-year plan to finish the war in 1975–76
- **Tran Van Hai** · Sergeant Major, senior drill sergeant of the drill-sergeant academy at the Quang Trung Training Center from 1957, a member of its first class · invented in 009 · Trained the drill sergeants of the ten-week course and kept the rule that a recruit takes his orders from a Vietnamese
- **Raymond F. Coyle** · Lieutenant Colonel, US Army; chief of the MACV advisory detachment to the Central Training Command 1964–67 · invented in 009 · Watched and scored the centres and did not teach; backed the refusal of June 1964 to return a third of the drill sergeants to the battalions
- **Pham Van Tho** · Corporal, PF platoon at the Long Huu post, Kien Hoa, trained at the Ben Tre centre in 1962 · invented in 009 · Kept a third of the platoon's ammunition for the second assault on 17 November 1964 and brought his two wounded alive to the Ham Long boats
- **Walter K. Dorsey** · Lieutenant Colonel, USMC; commanding officer, 1st Battalion, 26th Marines, 1965–67 · invented in 006 · Brought the battalion to Dien Ban as a unit; held the breach at La Huan in August 1966; reported in August 1967 that his Marines "do everything right in a firefight and are given none" after a year in which mines killed two-thirds of his dead
- **Vu Ngoc Hoan** · Colonel, commander of the ARVN 25th Division from March 1967 · invented in 007 · The list's second name; moved the dependents' quarters inside the perimeters and paid the ration in rice after the devaluation of 1966, cutting the division's desertion by about a quarter
- **Lam Quang Phuc** · Master Sergeant First Class, senior instructor of the National NCO Academy at Dong De from 1957, a Tonkinese who came south with the Commando School in 1954 · invented in 010 · Taught every class from 1957; kept the academy's course whole through the mobilization and the National Day decree of 1970
- **William H. Rourke** · Master Sergeant, US Army; senior NCO adviser to the Dong De academy from 1963, a Korean War platoon sergeant · invented in 010 · Advised the Vietnamese faculty rather than teaching; his successor left in March 1971 and reported the course taught as he had found it

- **Nguyen Huu Tam** · Captain, commanding a company of the 3rd Battalion, 11th Regiment, 7th Division, in 1966 · invented in 010 · Wounded in the first minutes of the ambush at Hau My; returned to the battalion in March 1967
- **Raymond L. Ochoa** · Staff Sergeant, US Army; platoon sergeant in a battalion of the 196th Infantry Brigade at LZ Baldy in 1970 · invented in 010 · Took four men off the line for heroin and was wounded by a grenade rolled under his bunk on 14 March 1970

- **Nguyen Van Thieu** · President of the Republic of Vietnam · HIST · Refused the Dalat study on 11 March 1975 and gave no withdrawal order on 13–14 March; did not resign; took the study's method for SOUTHERN WALL in May 1975
- **Ngo Quang Truong** · Lieutenant General, commander of I Corps 1972–75 · HIST · Held I Corps in March–April 1975 on a plan that moved the soldiers' families first; withdrew Hue in order on 14–18 April and held Da Nang until SOUTHERN WALL

- **Tran Van Tra** · Lieutenant General, commander of the B-2 Front · HIST · In this war won the argument for Phuoc Long against a more cautious Politburo, and lost the argument of 31 March 1975 for a thrust on Saigon in May
- **Le Duan** · First Secretary of the Workers' Party of Vietnam · HIST · In this war cut back the autumn raids of 1974 and on 25–31 March 1975 put the offensive on Saigon back to the dry season of 1975–76
