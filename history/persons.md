# Recurring persons

People who recur in the chronicle, historical or invented. Appended by `scripts/fie.py` from persons blocks. A later entry for the same person updates the earlier one.

Format: `- **<Name>** · <role> · <origin: HIST or invented in <work>> · <one line>`
- **Truong Vinh Khoa** · Commissioner Against Corruption from 1 January 1964, formerly a judge of the Saigon Court of Appeal · invented in 001 · Refused to charge the Dalat generals without evidence, convicted a general in 1967, audited Phoenix from 1968, kept in office but placed under the presidential Inspectorate in 1971
- **Phan Duy Khiem** · Brigadier General, IV Corps deputy commander for logistics 1965–67 · invented in 001 · Convicted by the Special Court on 18 April 1967 of ration and fuel diversion and sentenced to eight years
- **Vo Dinh Cuong** · Director General of National Police 1 January 1964 – 12 March 1966, formerly an inspector of the Sûreté · invented in 002 · Detained in Khanh's coup and restored in three days, kept the police out of the September 1964 coup, removed by Ky's decree in March 1966 and replaced by Loan
- **Dinh Quang Tuyen** · Judge of the Court of Cassation, chairman of the Provincial Administration Selection Board from 1 January 1964 · invented in 002 · Published the first list on 27 April 1964; the Board he chaired outlived four governments and was packed by Thieu in 1970
- **Ha Thuc Lan** · Lieutenant Colonel, engineer officer, province chief of Long An from June 1964 · invented in 002 · Placed third in the first examination; planned Operation NIGHT LANTERN with the rule that no post was settled without its platoon and a reaction within the hour
- **Le Cong Phuong** · Major, district chief of Can Giuoc from 1964, a native of Can Duoc · invented in 002 · Co-planned NIGHT LANTERN and returned the 1964 titles to their families in April 1966
- **Tran Van Sang** · Major, district chief of Mo Cay, Kien Hoa, June 1964 – November 1965 · invented in 002 · Killed by a command-detonated mine on 14 November 1965, the Front's "most harmful" district chief in the province
- **Harold Kessling** · Major, later Lieutenant Colonel, USAF; chief of the radar and imagery exploitation cell at Tan Son Nhut from 1964 · invented in 003 · Had the watch on the night of 4 August 1964 and reported no boats near the destroyers; built the Sihanoukville count night by night from 1965 to the joint estimate of March 1967
- **Arthur L. Venn** · Lieutenant Colonel, US Army; chief of the MACV small-arms new-equipment training team from January 1964 · invented in 004 · Ran Operation IRON GATE under the rule that no new weapon left a depot until the old one came in and no carbine went to a post unzeroed; refused to shorten the course for the corps commanders
- **Lam Van Thien** · Lieutenant Colonel, province chief of Chuong Thien in 1966 · invented in 004 · Protested in writing the corps' borrowing of his Regional Force companies in April 1966; his returns of the posts lost became the case for the 1967 rule that RF companies belong to their province
- **Truong Vinh Khoa** · Commissioner Against Corruption from 1 January 1964, formerly a judge of the Saigon Court of Appeal · invented in 001 · Refused to charge the Dalat generals without evidence, convicted the Sa Dec ammunition ring in 1967 on cartridge lot numbers and a general the same year, audited Phoenix from 1968, kept in office but placed under the presidential Inspectorate in 1971
- **Edwin T. Sorrell** · Major, later Lieutenant Colonel, USAF; chief of the coordinate cell at Tan Son Nhut from March 1964 to its disbandment in September 1973, formerly a geodesist of the Aeronautical Chart and Information Center · invented in 005 · Found the map datum error and ruled that no map coordinate would be given to a guided bomb; built the folders of 1964–65 and ran the siege box at Con Thien, Khe Sanh, Lam Son 719, An Loc and Kontum
