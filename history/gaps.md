# Gap register

What the force lacks, from the Theater Needs Assessment and checkpoints. Appended by `scripts/fie.py` from gaps blocks. A later line for the same gap updates its status.

Format: `- **G-<n>** · <name> · <status> · <one line> (<work>)`
- **G-1** · A state without a foundation · open · A junta two months old, an administration gutted by the coup, and a government that offers the peasant the landlord's return; no claim on the countryside (TNA)
- **G-2** · Who holds the countryside after dark · open · The government holds the hamlets by day from district towns and posts; the Viet Cong hold them by night, and isolated posts arm the enemy when they fall (TNA)
- **G-3** · Identifying the infrastructure inside the population · open · No count, no rural police, no records, no fused intelligence on the VCI; an enemy government inside the hamlets that we cannot name (TNA)
- **G-4** · The officer corps: selection, leadership, desertion, corruption · open · Officers chosen for class, education and loyalty rather than leadership; desertion heading for 73,000 a year; corruption in pay, supply and office (TNA)
- **G-5** · The American political clock · open · Tolerance finite and of unknown size, spent by casualties, cost, time and exposed false reporting; the state must be ready before it expires (TNA)
- **G-6** · Advisory leverage and its limits · open · Advisers without authority, of falling quality, in a divided mission; real leverage costs the Republic its legitimacy (TNA)
- **G-7** · Finding and fixing the main force · open · A growing main force that chooses whether to fight, unseen under canopy and at night, and gone before blocking forces arrive (TNA)
- **G-8** · Sanctuary and the theory of attrition · open · Laos, Cambodia and the North let the enemy control his losses and regenerate beyond reach; attrition has no crossover point (TNA)
- **G-9** · The Trail: throughput required against capacity · open · A small external requirement, about 20 tons a day, against an elastic system; interdiction counts only as tonnage arriving below requirement (TNA)
- **G-10** · The measurement problem · open · Reporting inflated where it flatters and undercounted where it alarms; body count, hamlet counts and infiltration all wrong in a consistent direction (TNA)
- **G-11** · The sea and Sihanoukville · open · An unwatched coast, a seaborne infiltration group since 1959, and a Cambodian port beyond reach that can open at Hanoi's request (TNA)
- **G-12** · Air power against a dispersed, night-moving enemy · open · Few aircraft, slow clearance, no night capability and no sensors to find targets worth striking; the right targets are posts under attack at night (TNA)
- **G-13** · No single manager of the war · open · American agencies and Vietnamese ministries each run their own programmes; pacification belongs to no one (TNA)
- **G-14** · No lever on Hanoi's will · open · No authorised instrument reaches the Politburo's decision; the best lever is a South that is visibly not collapsing (TNA)
- **G-15** · The theater base · open · One congested port, cut roads and railway, scarce helicopter hours and few engineers; any large provision outruns the base (TNA)
- **G-16** · Personnel spaces · open · No ceiling is set, so nothing is foreclosed by number, but every space costs pipeline, rotation, port capacity, legitimacy and the clock (TNA)
