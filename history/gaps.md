# Gap register

What the force lacks, from the Theater Needs Assessment and checkpoints. Appended by `scripts/fie.py` from gaps blocks. A later line for the same gap updates its status.

Format: `- **G-<n>** · <name> · <status> · <one line> (<work>)`
