# Settled positions

Judgments the analysis has committed to (E3). Appended by `scripts/fie.py` from positions blocks; never edited. A position is changed only by a reversal entry that names it.

Format: `- **POS<work>-<n>** · vars: <variable ids> · <the judgment> (<where it was made>)`
Reversal: `- **REV<work>-<n>** · reverses POS… · vars: … · <the new judgment and what changed>`
- **POSTNA-1** · vars: GVN.political_stability, POP.gvn_legitimacy, POP.land_tenure · The binding deficit on the opening date is political: no provision that leaves the Saigon government's standing in the countryside unchanged moves the end state above rung (b), whatever it does to the enemy's forces (TNA G-1)
- **POSTNA-2** · vars: POP.night_control, RFPF.effectiveness · Night control of a hamlet is held only by security resident in it with a means of calling help and a reaction that arrives in the dark; mobile forces that leave at dusk do not change it (TNA G-2)
- **POSTNA-3** · vars: ENEMY.engagement_control, ENEMY.regeneration, SANCT.laos, SANCT.cambodia · While the enemy controls whether to accept battle and regenerates in sanctuary, attrition has no crossover point; a strategy that waits for one waits for something the geometry does not permit (TNA G-8)
- **POSTNA-4** · vars: TRAIL.capacity, TRAIL.tonnage_arriving · On the opening date the enemy's external requirement from the North is of the order of 20 tons a day against a Trail of 20–30 tons a day and an uncounted sea route; Trail capacity is elastic with labour, so interdiction is judged only by tonnage arriving below the requirement, never by trucks or porters destroyed (TNA G-9)
- **POSTNA-5** · vars: US.political_tolerance, US.credibility_gap, US.casualties · The American political clock is spent by casualties, cost and time, and fastest by the discovery that reported progress was false; a provision that improves a metric without improving the war shortens the clock (TNA G-5, G-10)
- **POSTNA-6** · vars: ADVISE.leverage, POP.gvn_legitimacy · Advisory leverage trades short-run effectiveness against long-run legitimacy; no position on that line is free, and every proposal to increase leverage states what it costs the Republic's claim to be its own government (TNA G-6)
- **POS001-1** · vars: POP.land_tenure, POP.gvn_legitimacy, POP.night_control · Land reform changes what the government's arrival means, not whether it arrives: its value in any hamlet equals the night security behind it, and titles issued are never evidence of allegiance (001 §2 F001-4, F001-10; §4.P2)
- **POS001-2** · vars: GVN.corruption, ADVISE.leverage, GVN.political_stability · An independent anti-corruption body in the Republic survives only with a Vietnamese patron and an American lever; its durable effect is on muster, pay and documentary cases below the top, and its reach upward ends when the lever lapses with the American drawdown (001 §4.P4)
- **POS001-3** · vars: HANOI.will · Reform that erodes the Front's base raises Hanoi's urgency and shifts the arguments inside the Politburo; it does not change Hanoi's commitment to armed reunification or the timing of its decisions to invade (001 §2 F001-12; §4.P2)
- **POS001-4** · vars: POP.gvn_legitimacy, VCI.strength · A one-band gain in the countryside's allegiance does not carry into the conventional battles of 1972 and 1975; the end state turns on the military balance after the American withdrawal, and political gains are necessary but not sufficient to move it (001 §4.N)
- **POS002-1** · vars: GVN.police, FIND.vci, POP.night_control · A rural police and Special Branch attack the infrastructure, not the main force; their value in any village equals the night security beside them, and where they work best their arrest count falls, so arrest counts and quotas are never the measure of them (002 F002-11, F002-17; §4.P2)
- **POS002-2** · vars: GVN.admin_capacity, ADVISE.leverage, GVN.political_stability · Merit selection of province and district chiefs survives the coups only with the American aid lever behind it; when the lever lapses with the drawdown the President captures the Board without abolishing it, and its durable gain is the chiefs' experience below the palace (002 §4.P4, EV002-6)
- **POS002-3** · vars: GVN.police, GVN.political_stability, POP.gvn_legitimacy · A stronger and better-recorded police in a coup-prone state is used by the government of the day against its domestic opposition, and its records are a list of loyalties the enemy can capture; both costs are paid and do not outweigh the gain in the held belt (002 F002-9, F002-16; §4.P2, §4.P3)
- **POS003-1** · vars: FIND.main_force, FIX.capability, STRIKE.responsiveness · Airborne radar and signals reconnaissance find the enemy's movement in the open and his headquarters' transmitters, never his regiments under canopy; their value in any engagement equals what can be put on a track within the hour, and an improvement in finding is never claimed as an improvement in fixing (003 §2 F003-5; §4.P2–P5)
- **POS003-2** · vars: INFIL.sea, SANCT.cambodia, TRAIL.tonnage_arriving · In this war the sea route to the South's coast closed in 1964 under observation and the enemy's southern supply moved to Sihanoukville from June 1965; supply through Cambodia, not by sea, is the principal external line into III and IV Corps from 1965 until Lon Nol's coup (003 §2 F003-3; EV003-2)
- **POS003-3** · vars: US.political_tolerance, US.credibility_gap · In this war no attack of 4 August 1964 was claimed and Pierce Arrow was not flown; the Southeast Asia Resolution rests on the attack of 2 August, and the Tonkin deception is not part of the credibility gap in any later phase (003 §4.P1, §4.P3; EV000-3)
- **POS004-1** · vars: RFPF.effectiveness, POP.night_control, ENEMY.engagement_control · Infantry small arms change how the fights the enemy accepts go, never which fights he accepts; at the outpost their value equals the platoon's presence after dark and the reaction behind it, and the enemy's answer to a better-armed post is to attack what is not rearmed (004 F004-1, F004-3, F004-16; §4.P1)
- **POS004-2** · vars: ARVN.effectiveness, US.unit_quality, US.political_tolerance · In this war the ARVN and the RF/PF carry modern carbines from 1964, and the 1967 M16 crisis and inquiry and the 1968–69 rearmament do not occur; from 1969 the historical army has caught up, and the remaining difference is the squad gun, the marksman and the practice (004 F004-6; §4.P2–P4)
- **POS004-3** · vars: ARVN.self_sufficiency, LOG.distribution · Abundant American ammunition from 1964 deepens the RVNAF's dependence on volume of fire; the 1974 ration falls hardest on the squad gun, and the divisional hoards built against it are lost in the collapse of March 1975 (004 F004-15; §4.P6)
- **POS005-1** · vars: STRIKE.responsiveness, FIND.main_force, FIX.capability · GPS weapons change what dies at a measured coordinate, never what can be located; in this war the J's precision is decisive where the enemy holds still long enough to be measured, at the siege and the fixed crossing, and adds nothing against the main force under canopy or the infrastructure in the hamlets (005 §2 F005-1, F005-7, F005-21)
- **POS005-2** · vars: ARVN.self_sufficiency, FIX.capability, US.political_tolerance · In this war the defence of besieged positions from 1965 rests on the B-52J and an American coordinate cell; it is far stronger while American air power is present and leaves the RVNAF more dependent on it when it goes (005 §2 F005-20)
- **POS005-3** · vars: ENEMY.engagement_control, FIX.capability · In this war from Khe Sanh onward the PAVN does not lay a set-piece siege to a position the Americans can measure, until the conventional offensive of 1972 forces it to besiege An Loc and Kontum; it loses that one way of choosing its battles while the B-52J and the coordinate cell are present, and regains it when they leave in 1973 (005 §4.P3, §4.P5; EV000-41, EV000-76, EV000-77)
- **POS005-4** · vars: TRAIL.capacity, TRAIL.tonnage_arriving · In this war the panhandle's fixed crossings were cut by the B-52J from April 1966, and Group 559 rebuilt the net as braided fords, causeways and pontoons with no single point of failure; from 1966 the Trail is more redundant than history's, and precision strikes on crossings are never counted as reductions in tonnage arriving (005 §4.P2; EV005-2, EV000-55)
- **POSCP01-1** · vars: US.political_tolerance, US.credibility_gap · The small gains on the American clock in memoranda 001–005 (no Tonkin deception, honest damage assessment, no M16 scandal, a conviction in 1967, fewer prisoners and no Bach Mai in 1972) narrow the credibility gap and do not move tolerance by a band in any phase, because the clock was spent by the casualties of 1965–68 and by Tet, which no provision changed and one made sharper (CP01 §3; C003-3)
- **POSCP01-2** · vars: ADVISE.leverage, ARVN.self_sufficiency · In this war the American lever was used to protect the Republic's honest institutions from its own governments and never to make the Republic own the instruments of the conventional war (coordinates, reconnaissance, guided weapons, ammunition stocks); the dependence deepened by memoranda 004 and 005 was never contested by the instrument that could have contested it (CP01 §3)
- **POSCP01-3** · vars: ARVN.leadership, GVN.admin_capacity · The Commission, the Selection Board and every provision that needs a competent leader draw on one pool of perhaps 900–1,200 honest, able field-grade officers in 1964–66; the pool is spent several times over and the divisions are the loser each time, which is why G-4 stays open while the provinces improve (CP01 §3)
- **POS006-1** · vars: FIND.vci, VCI.strength, POP.night_control · Phung Hoang adds the layer above the hamlet to what the Special Branch knows, only where the night is held; outside the held belt it is the historical Phoenix whatever its rules, and its audited count, which falls where it works best, is the measure, never its arrest count (006 F006-15, F006-18, F006-26)
- **POS006-2** · vars: US.unit_quality, US.casualties, ENEMY.engagement_control · Unit rotation makes the American battalion fight better in the contacts it gets and changes neither which contacts it gets nor the late-war breakdown of discipline; it gives the enemy a relief window to aim at, and it does not prevent atrocity (006 F006-6, F006-16, F006-19)
- **POS006-3** · vars: ADVISE.leverage, ARVN.self_sufficiency · The selected advisory corps deepens influence, not authority; its durable values are continuity across the American unit's relief and honest reporting, and a better adviser deepens the advised unit's dependence on American fire unless he hands over the radio, which works for artillery, the VNAF and medevac and not for the B-52J (006 F006-2, F006-17, F006-23)
