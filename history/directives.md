# Standing directives

Corrections to method and permanent additions to the analysis's attention, issued by meta-checkpoints (B3) and checkpoints. Appended by `scripts/fie.py`; every memorandum loads all of them.

Format: `- **DIR<work>-<n>** · <directive> (<why>)`
- **DIRCP01-1** · In each Part 3, take at least one causal chain from an earlier memorandum whose later links fall in a phase this memorandum revises, and test it against the war as now written; where the new provision or free play makes it resolve differently, say so in the chain review and let it (every chain in memoranda 001–005 resolved as predicted, which is more likely tidiness than foresight)
- **DIRCP01-2** · A war-wide figure for lives saved, or any war-wide count built from a rate, must be shown at work in at least two dated events in the chapters before a later memorandum repeats it; until then it is cited as a judgment resting on its rate, with the rate named (CP01 calibration of the [X-004] lives-saved figure)
- **DIRMT01-1** · In every §4.N, state the date of the Republic's fall as its own estimate, separately from the rung: a window, the mechanisms in the cumulative force that pull it earlier and those that pull it later, and which dominate and why; "on the historical timeline" may be written only as the conclusion of that test, and a change of date is reported as a change of date and does not by itself move the rung between (a) and (b) while the ladder's overlap stands (the historical fall, twenty-five months after the last American combat troops left, satisfies the words of rung (b), and seven memoranda have kept (a) and (b) apart by the date alone)
- **DIRMT01-2** · In every §4.N, carry a running sum of the sub-band estimates that all memoranda so far have made on the same variable (American dead, ARVN battalions not lost, and the like), tagged as exercise judgments with their rates named, and say whether the sum crosses a band; and treat the American aid appropriation of 1974 and the refusal of April 1975 as events a provision can change, not as history to be carried (each memorandum judged its own saving of American lives alone, and the 1974 vote was never examined)
- **DIRMT01-3** · Where a chapter's end vector returns a variable to the historical state from a non-zero band in the phase before, the chapter must contain the dated events that produce the convergence; and in every memorandum that revises Phase VI, state in the phase's analysis the Republic's state at the end of 1974, before the last offensive, because the phase's end vector is measured after the fall and cannot record anything the fall erased (Phase V returned four countryside variables to history with no sentence on the countryside, and Phase VI's vector is blind by construction)
- **DIRMT01-4** · Where a memorandum alters an event and keeps its historical outcome, name what absorbs the difference (the enemy's choice to bypass or decline, a decision above the level the provision reached, an external fixed by law or by another government) and show it in the event; "as historically" is a conclusion, never a mechanism (in Phase VI at least six altered events in the last campaign restore the historical outcome by statement)
- **DIRMT01-5** · Where a perverse effect or a capture undoes a gain, state the net in both directions with its weight, and where several provisions' institutions fall to the same mechanism in the same years, judge them together before judging each alone (four institutions from four memoranda fall to the lapse of the American lever in 1970–71, each judged alone, and every memorandum closes on the gain undone)
- **DIRMT01-6** · The next memorandum or review whose Part 3 revises Phase V or VI states the PAVN's 1972 losses in this war against the historical figure and the rebuilding time that follows, before writing 1973–75 (memorandum 005 made the measured sieges the enemy's worst battles; Phase V keeps the historical losses and Phase VI the historical rebuilding)
- **DIRMT01-7** · Every memorandum whose Part 3 revises Phase V or VI tests its effects against the critical path of 1975 (the palace's orders of 13–14 March, the 1st Corps' march against the rains, and Minh's surrender) and states whether any mechanism could delay the collapse of I and II Corps by about three weeks, the threshold beyond which the assault falls into the rains; effects on fights the PAVN could bypass are reported as such (the date of the fall was carried for seven memoranda without its critical path ever being stated)
- **DIRMT01-8** · Every memorandum touching the countryside states its effect separately for the Delta, the coastal lowlands of I and II Corps, the Highlands and their peoples, and the Saigon belt, and names a place in each where the effect is shown or says why there is none (the Delta was written as a footnote, the Montagnards appear twice and FULRO never, and Binh Dinh vanished from the record after 1967)
- **DIRMT01-9** · Every provision touching the reliability of the ARVN or the territorial forces addresses the soldier's family: where it lives, what his pay buys after inflation, and what happens to it when the unit moves or the front gives way (the corps of 1975 dissolved toward their families, and no memorandum had treated them)
- **DIRMT01-10** · The enemy's decisions have authors: in each memorandum name the body or commander who took the enemy's decision of G3, and distinguish Hanoi, the B-2 Front and COSVN, and the regional fronts where they differed; treat the Soviet and Chinese supply as variables with their own politics (COSVN vanishes from the record after 1971, the Phuoc Long argument is told as one mind, and the Soviet deliveries of 1974 are absent)
- **DIRMT01-11** · Every memorandum touching the countryside or the RVNAF states its effect on rice (production, procurement and the Front's take) and on the piaster and the soldier's pay, with at least one dated event (rice appears eleven times in Phase II and once in 1972–75, the years the economy decided)
