# E04 — The Regional Forces at STILL WATER and Bau Bang carried BARs, not M249s
memos: 006, 007, 009
location: history/chapters/P1.md EV006-2 (Operation STILL WATER, written by memorandum 006, memos/006/amend.md), the paragraph on the operation's plan; history/chapters/P2.md EV007-3 (Operation OPEN ROAD, the Bau Bang convoy paragraph written by memorandum 009, memos/009/amend.md)
wrong: EV006-2: "the Regional Forces companies of Hoc Mon and Go Vap, with M4A1 carbines and their M249s, held the cordons at night"; EV007-3: "the second company, at the rear, put its two M249s into the treeline within the first two minutes [M009] [X-009]"
correct: EV006-2: the Regional Forces companies of Hoc Mon and Go Vap held the cordons "with M4A1 carbines and the BARs that the territorials kept as their automatic rifles" [M004]; EV007-3: the rear company "put its BARs into the treeline within the first two minutes", and the Regional Forces never drew the squad gun [M004]. The Regional and Popular Forces received the M4A1 carbine of P004a but not the M249 of P004c: P004c's negative specification reads "The Regional and Popular Forces do not receive it: the outpost keeps its BAR" (memos/004/part1.md §1, P004c, "What it is not") [M004], and the province chiefs' diversion of squad guns to their RF posts was abandoned and the guns recalled to the squads (EC004-10, memos/004/part2.md) [M004]. EV000-42 already keeps the rule: the 311th RF Company at Cai Lay had "one squad gun lent by the 7th Division". No later memorandum issued the M249 to the territorial forces; memorandum 008's arsenal provision still lists the Browning Automatic Rifle "still with the territorial forces" (memos/008/part1.md) [M008].
source: Study ST02, us-and-arvn-small-arms-and-individual-weapons-ag (studies/us-and-arvn-small-arms-and-individual-weapons-ag.md), Questions for the record, Q-1 · ERRATUM; checked against memos/004/part1.md (P004c negative specification), memos/004/part2.md (EC004-10), history/chapters/P3.md (EV000-42) and memos/008/part1.md.
consequences: Both errors reached the war, so revision R04 corrects the two events in history/chapters/P1.md and P2.md. Neither event's outcome rested on the squad gun. STILL WATER was a police and Special Branch operation in which the RF companies held cordons; nothing in it turns on the cordon's automatic weapon. At Bau Bang memorandum 009 credited the lower RF losses (about 32 dead rather than about 40) to the six-week territorial course, which taught the men to leave the trucks on the far side, fight from the ditch and return fire in pairs [M009] [X-009]; that judgment stands with BARs, whose 20-round magazine gave the rear company a shorter burst of covering fire than a belt-fed gun would have. No end vector, position or end state changes. The small arms study's Bau Bang passage (§ on Phase II) is corrected by R04's study entry. Any later pack that loads the cards of memoranda 006, 007 or 009 loads this erratum.
