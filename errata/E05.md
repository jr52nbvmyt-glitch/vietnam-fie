# E05 — The National Police and the Police Field Force keep their historical weapons
memos: 002
location: memos/002/part1.md §1.1 (P002a) D3 · Weapons and payload, the paragraph "**Weapons.**"
wrong: "the 2026 configuration replaces the carbine with the M4 carbine and adds a 12-gauge pump shotgun of Remington 870 class to each constable's equipment, because the policeman's weapon must be good enough to defend the post against a probe and no better, or he becomes a soldier [U]. Each post has an M60 machine gun for the post's defence [U], where the historical police had at best an M1919A4 [S1]. The Field Force platoon carries M16-family rifles, an M79 grenade launcher and a machine gun, with 24 shotguns in each company's pool [S5] [U: M16A1 rather than the historical Garands and carbines from the start], and no mortars [S5]."
correct: The National Police and the Police Field Force keep their historical weapons [U: the user's ruling of 25 Nov 2026, "Police keep historical weapons for now"]. The village constable carries the M1 or M2 carbine, or a pump shotgun of the period where his station issues one, and a .38 revolver (the Smith & Wesson Model 10 or the M1917), with the M1911A1 pistol for officers and the M3A1 sub-machine gun in station armouries [M002·S1]. The post has no 2026 weapon: its only automatic weapon, where it has one, is the automatic rifle of the period, with at best an M1919A4 [M002·S1], which is what memorandum 002's own Part 2 counts in a post ("about ten carbines and an automatic rifle", §2 Part B, Perverse effect) [M002]. The Police Field Force carries the M1 and M2 carbine, the M1918A2 automatic rifle and the M79 grenade launcher (memos/006/part1.md, P006 PRU weapons: "what the Police Field Force carries in this war") [M006], with the company's pool of 24 shotguns [M002·S5]. The rearmament of memorandum 004 does not reach them (P004a negative specification; F004-1; the P002a interaction line in memos/004/part2.md) [M004]. The clause on the policeman's weapon being "good enough to defend the post against a probe and no better, or he becomes a soldier" stands as the reason a constable is not armed as a soldier; it no longer justifies a 2026 weapon. Everything else in D3 stands. "For now": a later provision may rearm the police; until one does, these are its weapons.
source: Study ST03, us-and-arvn-small-arms-and-individual-weapons-ag (studies/us-and-arvn-small-arms-and-individual-weapons-ag.md), Questions for the record, Q-6 · ERRATUM, which found memorandum 002 Part 1 at odds with memorandum 004 and the chapters and left the choice to the user because P002a's weapons were stipulated [U]; the user's ruling of 25 Nov 2026 in the project chat. Checked against memos/002/part2.md and part3.md, memos/004/part2.md (F004-1, the P002a interaction line), memos/004/manifest.toml (links note), memos/006/part1.md and memos/006/amend.md, history/chapters/P1.md (EV002-2, EV004-1) and the study's own tables.
consequences: None in the war. The error was confined to memorandum 002 Part 1's D3 paragraph and never reached a chapter, an end vector, a card, a register or the end state: P002a's card (figures, negative specification, denial, verdict) states no weapons, memorandum 002's own Parts 2 and 3 already wrote the posts as stores of "M1 carbines and a radio" and "about ten carbines and an automatic rifle", with Long Dinh on 3 October 1964 "a gift of nine carbines to the Front's guerrillas" (F002-8), and every later record carries the historical weapons: memorandum 004 (P004a does "not cover the National Police, the Police Field Force"; F004-1, the attacks move to "the men who still carry carbines"), EV002-2 ("ten constables carrying the carbines of 1950"), EV004-1 (a third of the 290,000 old weapons handed down to the police, the Combat Youth and the militia over MACV's objection), memorandum 006 (the PRUs and the Field Force with carbines, M1918A2s and M79s), and the small arms study (ST03), whose table of history's weapons and of this war's rearmament leaves the police unchanged. Because the ruling keeps the version the war was written on, every outcome that rested on the police's weapons stands as written and needs no re-reading: the Front's choice of the police posts and the constables as the soft target from December 1964 (F004-1, F009-6), the roughly 300 weapons lost with the police posts by July 1965, nearly all of them carbines, and the constables' losses of 1964–65 (EV002-2) [X-004]. No revision is run, and no event, end vector, position or end state changes. Study ST03's Q-6 is answered by this erratum; the next checkpoint marks it RESOLVED. One related point is noted and not ruled on here: memorandum 002 gives the Field Force "no mortars" [M002·S5], while the Long An passage that memoranda 002 and 006 staged in their amend files gave the two Long An Field Force companies of November 1965 "60mm mortars" [X-002]; the current chapters no longer carry that clause, so nothing in the war rests on it. Any later pack that loads memorandum 002's card loads this erratum.
