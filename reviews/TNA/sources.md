# Sources — Theater Needs Assessment (work TNA)

One line per source, numbered in order of first use, consulted in this work's thread. The assessment cites them as [S1], [S2] … A bare [H] marks a fact so well established in the historical record that no single source is needed; [H] with a number would refer to the baseline's list in `history/baseline-sources.md`, which this work does not use.

[S1] *Foreign Relations of the United States, 1961–1963*, vol. IV, document 374: Memorandum from the Secretary of Defense (McNamara) to President Johnson, 21 Dec 1963 — https://history.state.gov/historicaldocuments/frus1961-63v04/d374 — "neutralization at best"; the new government "indecisive and drifting"; the country team "not working to a common plan"; "undue dependence on distorted Vietnamese reporting"; cross-border operations judged neither politically acceptable nor militarily effective on the scale proposed; adviser quality fallen since 1962
[S2] *FRUS 1961–1963*, vol. IV, document 369: Report on the Situation in Long An Province, Saigon, 20 Dec 1963 — https://history.state.gov/historicaldocuments/frus1961-63v04/d369 — 219 hamlets reported complete, no more than 35 capable; 147 armed squads reported, no more than 50 existing; 167 hamlets extensively damaged
[S3] *FRUS 1961–1963*, vol. IV, document 375: Letter from the Director of Central Intelligence (McCone) to President Johnson, 23 Dec 1963 — https://history.state.gov/historicaldocuments/frus1961-63v04/d375 — GVN statistics misleading; VC control more population and territory than estimated; delta and areas north of Saigon worse than expected; CIA intelligence previously through military channels
[S4] *FRUS 1961–1963*, vol. IV, document 366: Memorandum from the Director of the Defense Intelligence Agency (Carroll) to McNamara, 13 Dec 1963, "The Viet Cong Improved Combat Effectiveness and Insurgency Posture" — https://history.state.gov/historicaldocuments/frus1961-63v04/d366 — VC main force 21,000–23,000 as carried by COMUSMACV; irregulars 60,000–80,000; GVN forces about 500,000; VC battalions 30 (1962) to 37 (1963); five regimental headquarters; VC casualties about 27,000 including 20,000 KIA through November 1963, possibly overestimated; 914 known infiltrators in 1963; about 2,400 government weapons lost, 900 in November; incidents at an all-time high in November
[S5] *FRUS 1964–1968*, vol. I, document 392: Intelligence study prepared by Headquarters, MACV, 31 Oct 1964 — https://history.state.gov/historicaldocuments/frus1964-68v01/d392 — reported infiltration by year 1959–64 (about 6,200 in 1963; slightly over 34,000 for 1959 to Aug 1964; possibly as high as 45,000); method (164 infiltrator interrogations); Main Corridor through Laos; transport stations employing up to 6,000 men; no regular PAVN units identified
[S6] *FRUS 1964–1968*, vol. IV, document 36: Memorandum from McNamara to President Johnson, 24 Jan 1966, "The Military Outlook in South Vietnam" — https://history.state.gov/historicaldocuments/frus1964-68v04/d36 — external supply requirement of about 20 tons a day to sustain "1964" levels of activity; bombing's effect on infiltration capacity
[S7] "1963 in the Vietnam War" — Wikipedia, https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1963_in_the_Vietnam_War — US personnel at year end (15,894 on 31 Dec; high of 16,732–16,752 in October); NSAM 263 withdrawal of 1,000; McNamara's December visit and the 18 non-existent hamlets; Long An hamlets destroyed; US dead in 1963
[S8] "Battle of Ap Bac" — Wikipedia, https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_Ap_Bac — date, VC 261st and 514th Battalions (about 350), ARVN 7th Division force (about 1,400, 13 M113s, 15 helicopters), casualties, five helicopters lost, Harkins's claim of success, Vann's judgment, Cao's and Ba's conduct, Diem's reprimands for casualties
[S9] "Strategic Hamlet Program" — Wikipedia, https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Strategic_hamlet — 8,600 hamlets reported complete; about 20% meeting minimum US standards; Delta control about 20% at end of 1963; Long An three-quarters destroyed; Civil Guard crossing the river only by day in Vinh Long
[S10] "RVNAF strength" — GlobalSecurity.org, https://www.globalsecurity.org/military/world/vietnam/rvn-af-strength.htm — 1963 MAP-supported levels: regulars 225,000, Civil Guard 86,000, Self-Defense Corps 104,000; CIDG about 15,000 in 1962
[S11] "RVNAF desertions" — GlobalSecurity.org, https://www.globalsecurity.org/military/world/vietnam/rvn-af-desertions.htm — 73,000 desertions in 1964 and 113,000 in 1965 across all components; causes; divisions kept near home, so no mobile strategic reserve
[S12] "RVNAF leadership" — GlobalSecurity.org, https://www.globalsecurity.org/military/world/vietnam/rvn-af-leadership.htm — officer corps' social origin; advisers citing poor leadership as the foremost reason for ineffectiveness; unsatisfactory commanders seldom relieved; command turnover after the 1963 coup
[S13] "Ho Chi Minh trail" — Wikipedia, https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ho_Chi_Minh_trail — Group 559 from 1959; first truck unit December 1961; 1964 capacity of 20–30 tonnes a day; Group 759 with steel-hulled vessels
[S14] "Sihanouk Trail" — Wikipedia, https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sihanouk_Trail — seaborne share estimated as high as 70% before the Cambodian route; first Sihanoukville shipments October 1965; Unit K-20; 29,000 tons December 1966 – April 1969
[S15] "Vung Ro Bay incident" — Wikipedia, https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vung_Ro_Bay_Incident — about 100 tons of materiel landed from a single trawler, February 1965
[S16] *Cambodia: A Country Study* (Library of Congress), "Nonaligned Foreign Policy" — https://countrystudies.us/cambodia/18.htm — Sihanouk's termination of US aid in November 1963; break with Saigon August 1963; sanctuaries
[S17] *FRUS 1961–1963*, vol. IV, document 331: National Security Action Memorandum No. 273, 26 Nov 1963 — https://history.state.gov/historicaldocuments/frus1961-63v04/d331 — withdrawal objectives unchanged; concentration on the Mekong Delta; Cambodia "of the first importance"; planning for action against the North and for operations up to 50 km inside Laos
[S18] "Operation Farm Gate" — Wikipedia, https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Operation_Farm_Gate — Vietnamese crew requirement; 1st Air Commando Squadron (Composite) from 17 June 1963; approved strength of 41 aircraft and 474 men on 8 July 1963; B-26, T-28, SC-47
[S19] "Land reform in South Vietnam" — Wikipedia, https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Land_reform_in_South_Vietnam — Ordinance 57 (1956): 100-hectare retention; 422,000 ha acquired and 244,000 ha distributed 1958–61, under 10% of cultivated land; purchasers paid; Viet Minh distribution reversed and back rent demanded
